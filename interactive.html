Chip Advisor

NFL Big Data Bowl Paris Side Quest, by AWS, the NFL and Epitech, October 2026. Team of 3; the repository is hosted by a teammate.

We measured whether a tight end or a running back who "chips" a pass rusher before running his route protects the quarterback. On NFL Next Gen Stats tracking and PFF data for weeks 1–8 of 2021, a chip by an in-line tight end cut that rusher's pressure by 26 %. Sacks still happen, because the pressure then comes from another defender.

Our advice to the QB coach: look for the player who chipped at about 3 seconds. We delivered it as a small web tool with a coach briefing written by Mistral.

France Grid Forecast

First live forecast for 27 September 2026. Code

Every day a GitHub Actions job forecasts France's electricity consumption for each hour of the next day, then scores the past forecasts against what was really consumed.

RTE and weather data are stored as Parquet; dbt on DuckDB runs 19 data tests, and a failing test stops the run before anything is published. The gradient boosting model is retrained each week and replaces the current one only if it beats the naive baseline by at least 25 % on the last 8 weeks.

LoL Ratings 3.0

Personal project, 2026. The write-up is in French. Code

A rating of professional League of Legends players that accounts for the strength of their teammates and of their league: a ridge regression on each game's final gold difference (RAPM), with a box-score prior learned per role.

Built on the 10,044 pro games of 2025, then tested on the 2026 season, retrained each month. The Elo ratings it beats are updated after every game, which favours them.
